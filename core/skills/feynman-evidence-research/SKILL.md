# Feynman Evidence Research Methodology

## Core Invariants
1. **Never fabricate a source**: Every tool, project, paper, or dataset must have a verifiable URL.
2. **Never claim existence without verification**: Search before citing. If zero results, it does not exist.
3. **URL or it didn't happen**: No URL = omit claim.
4. **Read before summarizing**: Never infer paper contents from abstracts or titles alone.
5. **Mark status explicitly**: Direct observation vs multi-source inference vs unresolved gap.
